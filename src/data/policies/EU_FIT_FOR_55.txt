FIT FOR 55 PACKAGE (EU)

The Fit for 55 package aims to achieve the goal of reducing net greenhouse gas emissions by at least 55% by 2030.

Key components:
- Revision of the EU Emissions Trading System (EU ETS)
- Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM)
- Strengthened renewable energy targets (at least 40% by 2030)
- Increased energy efficiency targets
- Stricter CO2 emission standards for cars and vans
- Social Climate Fund for vulnerable households

Adopted: 2023
