INDIA'S NATIONAL ACTION PLAN ON CLIMATE CHANGE (NAPCC) - 2008

The NAPCC was published by the Prime Minister's Council on Climate Change. It aims to create awareness on the threat posed by climate change and outlines India's strategy for adaptation and mitigation.

Guiding Principles:
- Protecting the poor and vulnerable through inclusive and sustainable development.
- Achieving national growth through ecological sustainability.
- Devising efficient, cost-effective strategies for demand-side management.
- Deploying appropriate technologies for both adaptation and mitigation.
- Engineering new forms of market, regulatory and voluntary mechanisms.
- Implementing programmes with civil society and public-private partnerships.
- Welcoming international cooperation for technology transfer.

Eight Core National Missions:
1. National Solar Mission
2. National Mission for Enhanced Energy Efficiency
3. National Mission on Sustainable Habitat
4. National Water Mission
5. National Mission for Sustaining the Himalayan Ecosystem
6. National Mission for a Green India
7. National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture
8. National Mission on Strategic Knowledge for Climate Change

International Cooperation Goals:
- Minimizing negative impacts of climate change through adaptation.
- Ensuring fairness and equity in global actions.
- Upholding the principle of common but differentiated responsibilities.
