EU CLIMATE LAW

The European Climate Law makes the political commitment to climate neutrality by 2050 a legally binding obligation.

Key provisions:
- Reducing EU greenhouse gas emissions by at least 55% by 2030
- Establishing a European Scientific Advisory Board on Climate Change
- Mechanism for reviewing and adjusting EU climate policies
- Intermediate target for 2040 to be proposed by the European Commission

Entered into force: July 2021
