OCEAN ACIDIFICATION POLICIES

UN Sustainable Development Goal 14.3:
- Minimize and address impacts of ocean acidification
- Enhance scientific cooperation
- Support for ocean acidification observation networks

California Ocean Acidification and Hypoxia Mitigation Plan:
- Reduction of land-based nutrient pollution
- Protection and restoration of seagrass and kelp forests
- Ocean acidification monitoring network

Washington State Ocean Acidification Blue Ribbon Panel:
- Reduction of nitrogen loads from wastewater treatment
- Shellfish hatchery protection measures
- Coastal monitoring program expansion
