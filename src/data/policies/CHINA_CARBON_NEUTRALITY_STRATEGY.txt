CHINA'S CARBON NEUTRALITY STRATEGY (2025 White Paper)

China aims to achieve carbon peak before 2030 and carbon neutrality before 2060. This is a national strategy based on the "1+N" policy system, which is described as the world's most systematic carbon reduction policy framework.

Key Goals:
- CO2 emissions to peak before 2030.
- Achieve carbon neutrality before 2060.
- Non-fossil fuel share of primary energy consumption reached 19.8% in 2024.
- Coal share of energy consumption fell to 53.2% in 2024.
- By 2025, wind and solar power installed capacity target is over 1.2 billion kilowatts (already exceeded).

Key "Carbon Peak Ten Actions":
1. Energy Green and Low-Carbon Transition
2. Energy Efficiency and Carbon Reduction
3. Industrial Carbon Peak
4. Urban and Rural Construction Carbon Peak
5. Green and Low-Carbon Transportation
6. Circular Economy for Carbon Reduction
7. Green and Low-Carbon Technology Innovation
8. Carbon Sink Consolidation and Enhancement
9. Green and Low-Carbon People's Action
10. Regional Orderly Carbon Peak

Major Achievements by 2025:
- World's largest and fastest-growing renewable energy system.
- World's largest and most complete new energy industry chain.
- World's largest and fastest electric vehicle market (over 50% market penetration in 2025).
- Contributed approximately one-quarter of the world's newly increased greening area.
- The "New Three" (solar cells, lithium batteries, electric vehicles) are key export drivers.
