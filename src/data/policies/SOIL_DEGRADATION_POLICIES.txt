SOIL DEGRADATION POLICIES

EU Soil Thematic Strategy:
- Prevention of soil erosion, compaction, and contamination
- Maintenance of soil organic matter
- Soil sealing limitation in urban planning

Germany's Federal Soil Protection Act (BBodSchG):
- Preventive measures to avoid harmful soil changes
- Remediation requirements for contaminated sites
- Soil quality standards

China's Soil Pollution Prevention and Control Law (2019):
- Soil pollution risk management system
- Classification of agricultural land
- Polluter pays principle with strict liability
