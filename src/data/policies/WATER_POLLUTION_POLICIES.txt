WATER POLLUTION POLICIES

Clean Water Act (US):
- NPDES permit program for point source discharges
- Water quality standards
- Wetlands protection

EU Water Framework Directive (2000/60/EC):
- Integrated river basin management
- Good ecological and chemical status for all surface waters
- Public participation requirements

Safe Drinking Water Act (US):
- Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) for over 90 contaminants
- Source water assessment and protection
