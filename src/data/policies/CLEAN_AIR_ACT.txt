CLEAN AIR ACT (UNITED STATES)

The Clean Air Act is the comprehensive federal law that regulates air emissions from stationary and mobile sources.

Key provisions:
- Authorizes EPA to establish National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS)
- Requires states to develop State Implementation Plans (SIPs)
- Establishes emission standards for hazardous air pollutants
- Addresses acid rain through a cap-and-trade program
- Regulates vehicle emissions through tailpipe standards
- Protects the stratospheric ozone layer

Major amendments: 1970, 1977, 1990
