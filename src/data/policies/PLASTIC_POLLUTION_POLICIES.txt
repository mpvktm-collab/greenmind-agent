PLASTIC POLLUTION POLICIES

EU Single-Use Plastics Directive (2019/904/EU):
- Ban on plastic cutlery, plates, straws, cotton buds, polystyrene cups
- 90% separate collection target for plastic bottles by 2029
- 25% recycled content in plastic bottles by 2025

India's Plastic Waste Management Rules:
- Ban on single-use plastics (effective July 1, 2022)
- Extended Producer Responsibility for plastic packaging
- Minimum recycled content requirements

China's National Sword Policy (2018):
- Ban on import of post-consumer plastic waste
- Strict contamination limits (below 0.5%)
- Promotion of domestic plastic recycling
