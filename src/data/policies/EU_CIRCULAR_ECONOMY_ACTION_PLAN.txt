EU CIRCULAR ECONOMY ACTION PLAN

The EU Circular Economy Action Plan promotes sustainable product design and waste reduction.

Key measures:
- Making sustainable products the norm in the EU
- Empowering consumers for the green transition
- Reducing waste and increasing recycling rates
- Focusing on electronics, plastics, textiles, construction
- Introducing mandatory recycled content requirements
- Creating a right to repair for consumers
- Banning the destruction of unsold goods
