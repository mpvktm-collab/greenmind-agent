The European Green Deal is a set of policy initiatives by the European Commission with the overarching aim of making the European Union climate neutral in 2050. It was launched in December 2019. Key policies include:
- The European Climate Law which makes the climate neutrality goal legally binding
- The Fit for 55 package which aims to reduce EU emissions by at least 55% by 2030
- The Circular Economy Action Plan promoting sustainable product design
- The Biodiversity Strategy for 2030 protecting nature and ecosystems
- The Farm to Fork Strategy for sustainable food systems
