WASTE MANAGEMENT POLICIES

Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA) - US:
- Regulates solid and hazardous waste from cradle to grave
- Permit system for treatment, storage, and disposal

EU Waste Framework Directive:
- Waste hierarchy: prevention, reuse, recycling, recovery, disposal
- Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) principles
- 55% recycling target for municipal waste by 2025

Plastic Waste Management Rules - India:
- Ban on single-use plastics (effective July 2022)
- Extended Producer Responsibility for plastic packaging
