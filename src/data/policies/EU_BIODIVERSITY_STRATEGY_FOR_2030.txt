EU BIODIVERSITY STRATEGY FOR 2030

The EU Biodiversity Strategy aims to put Europe's biodiversity on the path to recovery by 2030.

Key targets:
- Legally protecting at least 30% of EU land and sea area
- Restoring 25,000 km of rivers to free-flowing status
- Reducing pesticide use by 50% by 2030
- Planting 3 billion additional trees by 2030
- Reversing the decline of pollinators
- Mobilizing at least €20 billion per year for nature
