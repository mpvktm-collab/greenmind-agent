NOISE POLLUTION POLICIES

Noise Control Act (US - 1972):
- Federal noise emission standards for products
- Aircraft and railroad noise regulation

Environmental Noise Directive (EU - 2002/49/EC):
- Strategic noise mapping for major roads, railways, airports
- Action plans to reduce noise levels
- Public information and consultation requirements

India's Noise Pollution Rules (2000):
- Ambient noise standards for industrial, commercial, residential, and silence zones
- Restrictions on loudspeakers and night time noise
