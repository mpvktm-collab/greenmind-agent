CLEAN WATER ACT (UNITED STATES)

The Clean Water Act regulates discharges of pollutants into U.S. waters.

Key provisions:
- National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) permit program
- Water quality standards for surface waters
- Regulation of wetland protection (Section 404)
- Funding for sewage treatment plants
- Nonpoint source pollution management programs
- Oil spill prevention and response requirements
