WATER POLLUTION HEALTH EFFECTS

Water pollution causes severe health problems in humans:

Waterborne Diseases:
- Cholera - caused by Vibrio cholerae bacteria in contaminated water, leads to severe diarrhea and dehydration
- Typhoid fever - bacterial infection from Salmonella typhi in contaminated water, causes high fever and intestinal bleeding
- Dysentery - caused by Shigella bacteria or amoeba, results in bloody diarrhea and abdominal pain
- Hepatitis A - viral liver infection from contaminated water, causes jaundice, fatigue, and liver damage
- Giardiasis - parasitic infection from Giardia lamblia, causes diarrhea, gas, and stomach cramps
- Cryptosporidiosis - parasitic infection causing severe watery diarrhea

Chemical Contamination Effects:
- Lead poisoning - causes developmental delays in children, kidney damage, and neurological problems
- Arsenic poisoning - leads to skin lesions, cancer, cardiovascular disease, and diabetes
- Nitrates in water - cause methemoglobinemia (blue baby syndrome) in infants
- Mercury contamination - causes neurological damage and developmental problems
- PFAS chemicals - linked to cancer, thyroid disease, and immune system problems

Vulnerable Populations:
- Children are most susceptible to waterborne diseases
- Elderly and immunocompromised individuals face higher risks
- Pregnant women risk birth defects from chemical contaminants

Prevention:
- Access to clean drinking water and sanitation
- Water treatment and filtration
- Regular testing of water sources
- Proper sewage treatment
