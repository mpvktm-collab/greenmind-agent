CLIMATE CHANGE IMPACTS

Climate change causes severe environmental and societal damage:

Temperature Related Impacts:
- Global average temperature increased 1.1°C since pre-industrial times
- More frequent and intense heatwaves causing heat-related deaths
- 2023 was the hottest year on record
- Arctic warming at 3-4 times global average
- Glacier melting and ice sheet loss accelerating

Extreme Weather Events:
- More intense hurricanes, cyclones, and storms
- Severe droughts affecting food and water security
- Heavy rainfall and flooding damaging infrastructure
- Increased frequency of wildfires destroying forests and property
- Category 4 and 5 storms becoming more common

Sea Level Rise:
- Global sea level rose 8-9 inches since 1880
- Projected rise of 1-4 feet by 2100
- Coastal erosion and flooding of low-lying areas
- Saltwater intrusion into freshwater aquifers
- Displacement of coastal communities (climate refugees)
- Loss of coastal wetlands and mangroves

Ecological Impacts:
- Loss of biodiversity and species extinction
- Ocean acidification harming marine life and coral reefs
- Coral bleaching events more frequent (Great Barrier Reef lost 50% of coral)
- Disruption of migration patterns and breeding cycles
- Forest dieback and reduced agricultural yields
- Polar bears, penguins, and other ice-dependent species threatened

Agricultural Impacts:
- Reduced crop yields (wheat, corn, rice)
- Changing growing seasons
- Increased pest and disease pressure
- Water scarcity for irrigation
- Food price volatility and food insecurity
- Estimated 30% reduction in global crop yields by 2050

Human Health Impacts:
- Heat-related illnesses and deaths
- Spread of vector-borne diseases (malaria, dengue, Zika)
- Respiratory problems from wildfire smoke
- Mental health impacts from climate anxiety
- Malnutrition from food scarcity
- Displacement and conflict

Economic Costs:
- $200-300 billion annual economic losses from climate disasters
- Infrastructure damage and repair costs
- Lost productivity and reduced economic growth
- Insurance industry losses
- Adaptation and mitigation costs
- Climate change could reduce global GDP by 10-20% by 2100
