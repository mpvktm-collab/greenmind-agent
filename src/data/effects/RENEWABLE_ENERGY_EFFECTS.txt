RENEWABLE ENERGY EFFECTS

Renewable energy provides positive environmental and economic benefits:

Carbon Emission Reduction:
- Solar power: 0.02 kg CO2 per kWh (compared to coal: 1.0 kg CO2)
- Wind power: 0.01 kg CO2 per kWh (98% less than fossil fuels)
- Hydropower: 0.02 kg CO2 per kWh
- Geothermal: 0.04 kg CO2 per kWh
- Biogas: 0.1 kg CO2 per kWh (70% less than natural gas)

Air Quality Improvement:
- Reduced particulate matter (PM2.5) by 40% in renewable-heavy regions
- No sulfur dioxide emissions (cause of acid rain)
- No nitrogen oxide emissions (cause of smog)
- No mercury emissions (neurotoxin)
- Estimated 3,000-10,000 premature deaths avoided annually per GW of coal replaced

Water Conservation:
- Solar PV uses 95% less water than coal power plants
- Wind power uses 99% less water than nuclear
- Coal plants consume 1,100 L/MWh of water
- Natural gas plants consume 400 L/MWh
- Renewable energy saves 4-5 trillion gallons of water annually

Land Use Impacts:
- Solar farms: 8-10 acres per MW
- Wind farms: 50-100 acres per MW (but only 2% occupied)
- Hydropower: reservoir flooding impacts
- Rooftop solar uses zero additional land
- Agrivoltaics: solar + agriculture on same land

Biodiversity Benefits:
- Reduced habitat destruction from mining (coal, uranium)
- No oil spills threatening marine ecosystems
- No thermal pollution of rivers (nuclear, coal)
- Reduced acid rain damage to forests
- Lower mercury contamination in fish

Economic Benefits:
- Renewable energy cost declined 90% in past decade
- Solar is now cheapest electricity source in history
- Wind power costs declined 70% since 2009
- Renewable energy jobs: 13.7 million globally (2022)
- $1.3 trillion annual investment in clean energy

Energy Independence:
- Reduced fossil fuel imports (energy security)
- Stable electricity prices (no fuel cost volatility)
- Distributed generation (rooftop solar, community wind)
- Reduced geopolitical conflicts over oil and gas
- Local economic development in rural areas

Challenges and Mitigation:
- Intermittency (solved with storage and grid integration)
- Mining for battery materials (recycling and alternative chemistries)
- Bird and bat collisions (curtailment, radar systems)
- Visual impacts (offshore wind, building-integrated solar)
- Recycling end-of-life solar panels and wind blades

Job Creation:
- Solar industry employs 4.9 million workers globally
- Wind industry employs 1.5 million workers
- Hydropower employs 2.5 million workers
- Renewable energy jobs grew 10% in one year
- Fossil fuel jobs declined 5% in same period

Global Renewable Capacity (2024):
- Solar: 1,600 GW installed
- Wind: 1,100 GW installed
- Hydropower: 1,400 GW installed
- Renewables provide 30% of global electricity
- Goal: 90% renewable electricity by 2050
