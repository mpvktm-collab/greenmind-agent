AIR POLLUTION HEALTH EFFECTS

Health Effects of Air Pollution:

Respiratory Effects:
- Asthma attacks and worsening of existing asthma
- Chronic bronchitis and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD)
- Reduced lung function in children and adults
- Respiratory infections including pneumonia
- Lung cancer

Cardiovascular Effects:
- Heart attacks and heart disease
- Stroke
- High blood pressure
- Irregular heartbeat (arrhythmias)

Other Health Effects:
- Premature death in people with heart or lung disease
- Low birth weight and premature birth
- Cognitive decline and neurological effects
- Diabetes

Vulnerable Populations:
Children, elderly, pregnant women, and people with pre-existing heart or lung disease are most vulnerable.

Protection Measures:
- Monitor AQI levels before outdoor activities
- Use air purifiers indoors
- Wear N95 masks on high pollution days
- Limit outdoor exercise when air quality is poor
